Do you need to change your Spotify account password? If you know your password, you'll need to use the Spotify website on your mobile or desktop browser. If you forgot or lost your password, you'll need to reset it.

Currently, there's no way to change or reset your password on the Spotify mobile app or desktop app, so you'll need to use a web browser. This wikiHow will show you how to change and reset your password using the Spotify website on your Windows, Mac, iPhone, or Android device. Things You Should Know

You can only change or reset your password in a web browser.

To change your password, log into the Spotify website. Click your profile → "Account" → "Change password".

To reset your password, open Spotify's Password Reset. Enter your email, then click the reset link sent to your email.

Spotify does not offer Two-Factor Authentication for ordinary users. This means it's impossible to add an extra layer of security to your account, so keeping your password safe is extremely important. Steps Method 1 Method 1 of 2: Changing Your Password Download Article 1
