Is your name in Teams incorrect, but you're not sure how to change it? Changing your Teams name depends on the type of account you're using. If you're using Teams on your work or school account, you likely won't be able to change it yourself. If you're using a personal Teams account, however, changing your name takes just a few clicks. In this article, we'll teach you how to change your Teams name. Keep reading to learn how! Changing Your Name in Teams

If you have a work account, you can't change your Teams name. You'll need to contact the team admin to have your name changed. If you have a personal account, click your profile picture in the upper-right corner, hover your name, and click the pencil icon to change your display name. Steps Section 1 of 3: Using a Work or School Account Download Article

Contact your administrator and ask for a name change. If you have an organization account, you can't change your own Teams name. You'll need to contact your Teams administrator and ask them to change the display name on your account.

If you're the Teams admin, open the account you want to edit in the M365 Admin Center. Click Manage contact information and change the "Display name" field to update the account's display name.[1]XResearch source Advertisement Section 2 of 3: Using a Personal Account Download Article 1

Click on your profile picture in the upper-right corner. If you don't have a profile picture, you'll click on the bubble with your initials. 2

Hover over your name. Note that you can only freely change the display name on your account if you have a personal account. If you're on a personal account, this menu will say "Personal" in the upper-left corner. 3

Click the pencil icon. This will open a dialog box. 4

Change your name. You must include a first name and a last name. Note that the changes will also be reflected in your Microsoft account, changing the name shown in your Outlook emails and other instances of your Microsoft account. 5

Click Save. Your new display name will be saved to your Teams account as well as your Outlook account.[2]XResearch source Advertisement Section 3 of 3: FAQ Download Article 1

Can I change my display name in one meeting? Yes, you can, if the meeting organizer has changed the meeting settings to allow participants to change their names. This will only change your name for this meeting; it won't change it in Teams overall.

To change your name in a meeting, click the People tab at the top of the screen, then hover over your name. Click the three-dot icon, then click Edit display name.
