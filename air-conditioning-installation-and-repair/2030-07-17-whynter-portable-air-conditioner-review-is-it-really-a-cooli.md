What is the Whynter ARC-14S 14,000 BTU dual-hose portable air conditioner?

This Whynter dual-hose air conditioner is a portable unit that features a maximum output of 14,000 BTUs. This size is suitable for rooms up to 500 square feet in size, although it did a great job cooling our somewhat larger area.

The portable AC unit has multiple settings, including cooling, fan-only, or dehumidifier modes, making it a versatile option. Portable AC units typically have “cool” and “fan” settings, but most lack the ability to serve as a dehumidifier, too.

Does the Whynter ARC-14S cool effectively?

I found that the Whynter air conditioner cools very quickly and is effective even during extremely humid conditions. I turned the unit on anytime our 600-square-foot room reached 80 degrees Fahrenheit. The unit was set to the lowest cooling setting of 61 degrees and the highest fan setting.

The Whynter instantly pushed cold air through the top vent, and the room was noticeably cooler within 10 minutes. After a half hour, the temperature had dropped down to 72 degrees, and after an hour, the room had reached its set temperature of 61 degrees Fahrenheit.

Although the manufacturer claims the unit is efficient for cooling up to 500 square feet, we found that it cooled our entire living/dining room area, which is about 1,000 square feet, to a comfortable temperature after a few hours. Photo: Stephanie Cronk for BobVila.com

Is the Whynter portable air conditioner right for you?

Where traditional portable air conditioners are bulky, this model is sleek stainless steel and slimmer than most. The design fits nicely into a modern home and also can tuck neatly into a corner if desired. I prefer the black and stainless steel over a traditional white unit, but that’s a matter of personal preference.

The only downside to this unit is that it is quite heavy and not as energy efficient as some other models. With that said, if you’re looking for a larger portable air conditioner, the Whynter ARC-14S may be right for you.

This powerhouse is ideal for larger rooms or living spaces and provides cooling comfort within minutes. I haven’t found many 14,000 BTU portable units that work as well as this one.
